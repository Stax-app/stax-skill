## THE 8 NON-NEGOTIABLES (apply to every video — a violation means it does not ship)

1. **Label every chart.** Any performance figure on screen carries a visible **"Backtest Result"** label. VO says **"would have"** — never "made," "worked," "returned," or "earned."
2. **No tickers.** No named stock, no specific price, no allocation, no price target. Ever.
3. **No income or return claims.** Credibility comes from process metrics (strategies built), never dollars earned.
4. **Replace every `[FILL IN]`** with a real, verified figure — or cut the claim. Never publish an example number.
5. **"Free" = building + paper trading only.** Live/automated deployment is the paid tier. Don't blur them.
6. **Caption carries the disclaimer:** "past performance doesn't guarantee future results."
7. **Buddi is a tool the user drives.** Never "we invest for you," never robo-advisor framing.
8. **CTA is comment-keyword only.** Never "link in bio" as the primary ask.

### BODY (clipped, ~5–6s per step, numbered overlays)
1. "One. The industry. Is it actually growing — and who dominates it?" — `1 · INDUSTRY`
2. "Two. The business. Where do the profits really come from, and who's it up against?" — `2 · BUSINESS MODEL`
3. "Three. The financials. Margins, return on equity, free cash flow per share, earnings growth — is it strong, and is it still climbing?" — `3 · FINANCIAL STRENGTH`
